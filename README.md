## ✦ Overview

The **VR Studio Sandbox** is a minimalist, high-ceiling 10×10×10 architectural studio room built for **Christopher** and **Antigravity**. It solves the challenge of live, in-headset spatial creation: enabling you to dynamically paint artwork onto a gallery wall canvas and instantiate 3D models onto a center display table in real time—**without ever taking off the headset or refreshing the browser**.

---

## 📸 Core Features

1. **The 10×10×10 Architectural Studio**:
   - Polished dark slate floor with perimeter brass bevel trim.
   - Coffered dark acoustic ceiling with directional gallery lighting.
   - 4-corner ambient accent illumination.

2. **The Dynamic Wall Canvas (North Wall)**:
   - High-resolution 4.2m × 2.6m stretched canvas with a dark walnut and brushed brass frame.
   - Dynamically updates with AI-generated paintings, atmospheric concepts, and chronicles on-the-fly without refreshing the page.

3. **The Model Inserter Table (Center Studio)**:
   - Minimalist dark charred oak workbench with tapered architectural steel legs.
   - Center circular bronze dais with a pulsating cyan tech/rune ring and focused overhead spotlight.
   - Dynamic 3D model loader that safely instantiates `.glb` models (Nexus Monolith, Antigravity Robot, Glimmer Hoverbot, Tesla Optimus PBR, Conservatory Flora) with smooth scale-in animations and continuous 360° inspection rotation.

4. **In-VR Push-to-Talk Command Terminal**:
   - Holographic glass terminal pedestal angled comfortably beside the workbench.
   - **Single-Click Push-to-Talk**: Push once to start listening, speak naturally into the Quest 2 microphone, and push again to dispatch your command.
   - **Quick Prompt Chips**: 1-click test chips for instant canvas swaps and 3D model insertions.
   - **Spoken Speech Feedback**: Speaks Antigravity's response directly into your Quest 2 headset speakers.

5. **Astra Head-Centric Comfort Locomotion**:
   - **Left Controller Stick**: Smooth sub-stepped walking with room perimeter and table collision sliding.
   - **Right Controller Stick**: Astra's head-centric rotation mathematics—pivoting about your tracked physical head position in room-space rather than the tracking origin, completely eliminating centrifugal arc disorientation.
   - **Laser Raycasters**: Trigger and point clickers on both controllers.
